1 Create Apple Developer Account

What: This lets you publish apps to App Store and TestFlight

Cost: $99/year (required by Apple)

Time: 5-10 minutes

💡 Important: You need this to publish ANY iOS app. It's Apple's requirement, not ours.

Steps:

  1. Go to developer.apple.com/programs
  2. Click "Enroll" → "Start Your Enrollment"
  3. Sign in with your Apple ID (same as App Store)
  4. Choose "Individual" (unless you're a company)
  5. Pay $99/year
  6. Wait for approval (usually instant, sometimes 24-48 hours)
✅ You'll know it worked when: You can access developer.apple.com and see "Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles"

2 Create Your App in App Store Connect

What: Register your app so Apple knows it exists

Cost: Free

Time: 3-5 minutes

Steps:

  1. Go to appstoreconnect.apple.com
  2. Click "My Apps" → "+" → "New App"
  3. Fill in:
    • Name: Your app name (e.g., "TaktikTräning")
    • Bundle ID: com.yourname.yourapp
    • SKU: Any unique text (e.g., "myapp2025")
    • Language: Your primary language
  4. Click "Create"
💡 Bundle ID Tip: Use format com.yourname.appname (e.g., com.john.taktiktraning). This CANNOT be changed later!

3 Create App-Specific Password

What: Special password for AppleDeploy to access your account

Cost: Free

Time: 2 minutes

Steps:

  1. Go to appleid.apple.com
  2. Sign in with your Apple ID
  3. Go to "Security" section
  4. Under "App-Specific Passwords" click "Generate Password"
  5. Name it "AppleDeploy" or "iOS Deployment"
  6. Copy the xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx password
  7. Save it somewhere safe!
✅ You'll get: A password like "abcd-efgh-ijkl-mnop" - this is what AppleDeploy uses
